P0 · Blocks launch
1. MLS® data licensing and display rulesSearch · Map · Listing · Compare
GTA listings come through PropTx data feeds. The brokerage has to sign a VOW Datafeed Agreement and follow the VOW rules. Sold history (used on Listing, Compare and Klyr AI) is VOW data and can only be shown behind a registered login. The rules also limit a search to 100 listings and require the listing brokerage on every listing, thumbnails included, in the same font and size.
Change in the designAdd "Listing brokerage: …" to search cards, map previews, compare columns and the listing header. Add VOW terms acceptance and email verification to account creation. Cap results at 100 with "Refine your search". Respect "no internet display" flags. Add board and MLS® attribution to the listing footer.
2. RECO advertising rules inside the appWelcome · Team · Login · every listing
RECO treats websites and social media as advertising. The brokerage must be identified clearly and prominently using its registered name, and agents must use their registered names and titles. Claims must be accurate and supportable.
Change in the designShow "Klyr Realty Inc., Brokerage" in the app header or footer on every screen, not just onboarding. Add the RECO title to agents ("Maya Chen, Salesperson"). Drop or substantiate the positioning claim "lowest all-in cost to buy in Ontario" anywhere it appears to the public. Get written consent before showing any sold price or testimonial.
3. Drip pricing and the all-in numberAll-in hero · Seller net · Pricing
The Competition Act treats leaving mandatory fees out of an advertised price as misleading. The only fees that can be left out are taxes imposed by federal or provincial law, such as HST. Klyr's whole position rests on the all-in number, so it has to hold up to that test.
Change in the designAdd HST and disbursements to the lawyer line, and show utility and fuel adjustments. Add an "Estimate as of [date] · assumptions" link under every all-in and net figure. Show a range where inputs are unknown. Keep "$999 + HST" as written, since HST is the permitted exclusion. List any Klyr admin or transaction fees in the price itself.
4. FINTRAC identity and recordsVisit · Offer · Seller prep · Deposit
Since October 1, 2025, agents must also verify the identity of unrepresented parties and record a third-party determination for them. Records are kept for five years. Since March 26, 2026, the maximum penalty is $20 million for an entity.
Change in the designExtend the ID flow with occupation, source of funds, a third-party question ("Are you acting for someone else?") and politically exposed person questions. Add an ID request flow for a self-represented buyer or seller on a Klyr deal. Add a receipt-of-funds record for each deposit. Add corporate buyer and beneficial ownership capture.
5. Deposits and wire-fraud protectionDeal · Errors
Under Form 100, "upon acceptance" means the deposit reaches the deposit holder within 24 hours. A brokerage then has five business days to put trust money in its real estate trust account. Real estate wires are a common fraud target, and the current design sends wire details in-app with no verification.
Change in the designAdd a deposit screen: deposit holder, trust account verified by phone call-back, a countdown to the 24-hour deadline, an upload for the receipt, and "Klyr will never change wire details by email". Add states for deposit received and deposit in trust.
6. Accessibility (AODA)All screens
Ontario organizations with 50 or more employees must make public websites and web content meet WCAG 2.0 Level AA. Organizations with 20 or more employees file compliance reports, with the next private-sector deadline on December 31, 2026. Klyr should design to WCAG 2.2 AA regardless of size.
Change in the designReplace #8E8E93 body text on white with #5E5E63 or darker. Set a 12 px minimum (11 px rule tags fail). Add visible focus rings and keyboard order. Give every map a list equivalent. Add captions and a transcript for video tours. Write alt text for photos. Label every icon button. Check 44 px touch targets on chips and steppers. Support larger text without clipping the money heroes.
7. Privacy, consent and credit checksAccount · Rental profile · Profile
MLS® data agreements also bind the brokerage to PIPEDA, Canada's anti-spam law and the Do Not Call list. The app collects ID, income, credit reports and landlord references.
Change in the designAdd a privacy notice at account creation. Get explicit consent before each credit pull, saying who receives the report. Add a "Who can see this" control on the rental profile. Make data download and deletion real flows with retention explained. Keep marketing consent separate from service messages, as it already is.
8. Klyr AI governanceAI sheet · AI picks
Klyr AI recommends lawyers, lenders and offers, and RECO holds the brokerage responsible for what clients are told. It needs the same controls as an agent's advice.
Change in the designSave every AI conversation to the client file. Add "Send to Maya to confirm" on any recommendation. Block price and negotiation advice to a party in multiple representation. Show sources for every figure it cites. Show a one-time notice on what data the AI sees. Never let the AI rank providers by what Klyr earns.
P1 · Flows a real transaction needs
Co-buyers and co-sellersInvite a spouse or partner. Each signs forms and verifies ID. The deal shows who still has to act.
Condo resaleForm 101, a status-certificate condition and lawyer review, reserve fund and special assessments in the all-in, and monthly fee changes.
New builds and assignmentsBuilder agreements, the cooling-off period for new condos, Tarion, HST relief eligibility, closing adjustments and occupancy fees.
Offer edge casesPre-emptive offers, bully offers, a condition on the sale of the buyer's home, Schedule B, amendments (Form 120), and a buyer's offer that is not accepted.
Deal falls apartConditions not met, a mutual release, deposit return, and a seller relist or buyer restart.
Showing systemReal availability, lockbox and tenant-occupied notice, a reschedule and cancel flow, and calendar sync.
Listing changesPrice change, extension, suspension and cancellation of the listing agreement, and terminated or relisted history.
Cash back payoutBank account verification, the lender disclosure letter, a receipt, and a clear path when commission is reduced or delayed.
Tenancy after move-inRent receipts, N-form notices, entry requests, maintenance, renewal and the end of the tenancy.
Agent handoffHow a consumer action shows up in Klyr Agent, agent availability, and a backup agent when Maya is away.
Account securityTwo-factor for signing and payouts, device list, session timeout, and account recovery without a passkey.
NotificationsPush permission prompt, quiet hours, SMS for deadlines inside 24 hours, and a notification for every irrevocable deadline.
P2 · Design quality
Component library. Money hero, breakdown list, task row, provider quote row, rule tag, chip, toggle and stepper are repeated by hand. Document each with variants and states.
Interaction states. Add hover, pressed, focus, disabled and loading for every button, plus skeletons for each data screen, not only search.
Iconography. Tab bar and bell have icons; rows, documents and providers don't. Pick one icon set from the design system.
Photography. Condos use house exteriors, and interiors are empty slots. Real listing photos come from the MLS® feed (up to 40 per listing on TRREB), so the gallery should be tested with 1 photo and with 40.
Dark mode and text size. v1 had dark mode; v2 dropped it. Test the money heroes at the largest text size.
Content review. A lawyer should review every "● Rule" note, form summary and AI disclaimer. Use one glossary (all-in, net, cash back, buyer's brokerage) everywhere.
Languages. Decide whether to add French and the GTA's largest languages. Forms stay in English; plain-language summaries could be translated.
Assumptions editor. Let users change the rate, down payment, lawyer and first-time status in one place, with every all-in updating.
Instrumentation. Define events for each journey step (search → visit → offer → firm → keys) so drop-off can be measured in testing.
