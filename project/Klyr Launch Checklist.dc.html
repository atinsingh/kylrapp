AccessibilityGrey text darkened to #5E5E63 on all screens. 12 px minimum text. Visible focus ring on every control. Reduced-motion support. Alt text on listing photos. Captions on the video tour. 44 px targets on all new controls.
Privacy and creditPrivacy notice required at sign-up. New Privacy and consent screen: separate marketing consent, approval for each credit check, rental profile visibility, data download, and account deletion with retention explained.
Klyr AIOne-time notice on what the AI sees. Conversations labelled as saved to the client file. “Send to Maya to confirm” after any answer. Every answer ends with sources. No price or negotiation advice once Form 326 is signed. Providers ranked by cost to the user.
