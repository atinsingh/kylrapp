Klyr’s 60–80% share is competitive, but the offer is crowded. Two rules shape how it can be shown. A cash-back rebate must be disclosed to the mortgage lender, and some lenders reduce the allowed price or down payment credit by that amount. RECO also treats steering buyers toward homes by commission as a breach of TRESA, so listings should never be ranked by cash back.

3. Ontario rules Klyr can build on

Offer transparency
Every buyer who submits an offer must be told how many competing offers there are. Sellers may choose to share price, conditions or deposit.
Buyer agreements
Buyers now sign a written agreement. If a listing offers less commission than agreed, the buyer may owe the difference.
FHSA + Home Buyers’ Plan
$8,000 a year up to $40,000 in an FHSA, plus up to $60,000 from an RRSP. Together that is $100,000 of tax-assisted down payment per person.

4. What competitors already do

Wahi offers co-buying with a partner, a Market Pulse view of over- and under-bidding, sold history and school data. Redfin lets buyers search by conversation, including commute and school questions, inside ChatGPT. HouseSigma focuses on sold prices and comparables. Properly helps people buy before they sell. None of them run the whole transaction after the offer the way Klyr’s journey does.

5. Proposed Klyr signatures

Lender-safe cash back
Auto-disclosed to your lender. You choose to put it toward closing costs, your mortgage, or your bank account.
Rent-to-own planner
Starts while you still rent. Tracks FHSA, RRSP, family gifts and expected cash back, and shows the month you can afford to buy.
Offer night, live
Shows how many offers are in, what the seller chose to share, and Klyr AI advice on whether to raise, hold or walk away.
Real monthly cost
Mortgage, tax, condo fees, energy and upkeep per month, side by side with your current rent. Suite income shown where a basement or garden suite is possible.
Buy together
Invite a partner or parents. Shared favourites, a gift letter, and co-signer paperwork in the same journey.
Commission check
On every listing, a plain yes or no: is the buyer commission covered, or would you owe a shortfall?
After the keys
Tarion or inspection follow-ups, a maintenance calendar, and a mortgage renewal alert. Klyr stays useful after closing.
